About AVMS

Learn about the Association of Veterinary Medical Students, ABU Zaria

Who We Are

The Association of Veterinary Medical Students (AVMS) is the official student body representing all veterinary students at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria. We are a community of passionate individuals dedicated to the advancement of veterinary medicine and the welfare of our members.

Our association serves as a platform for academic excellence, professional development, leadership cultivation, and community service. We bridge the gap between students and the veterinary profession, preparing our members for successful careers in veterinary medicine.

AVMS Community

Our Mission

To foster academic excellence, professional growth, and leadership development among veterinary students through quality programs, advocacy, and community engagement.

Our Vision

To be the premier veterinary student association in Africa, producing globally competent veterinarians who are leaders in animal health, food security, and public health.

Our Objectives

Our History

The Association of Veterinary Medical Students (AVMS) at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria was established to serve as the voice of veterinary students and to promote excellence in veterinary education.

Since its inception, AVMS has grown into a vibrant community of veterinary students dedicated to advancing the profession and serving the community. The association has organized numerous academic, social, and professional events that have enriched the student experience and prepared members for successful careers.

Today, AVMS ABU Zaria continues to be a leading force in veterinary student development, producing graduates who are making significant contributions to animal health, public health, and food security across Nigeria and beyond.
